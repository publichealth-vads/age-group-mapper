Age Group Mapper

Map an exact age and unit, as captured in NNDSS (PHVS_AgeUnit_NND) or syndromic data, to every CDC and WHO reporting scheme.

Data context
Starts with the commonly used NNDSS, Syndromic (ESSENCE), Mortality and Immunization schemes. Tick more to add them; Defaults restores this set.
References

Numbered sources cited on each scheme. Links open in a new tab. Accessed 2026-09-25.

    Rules applied. Age is normalized to days (1 year = 365.25 d, 1 month = 30.4375 d). Bands are lower-bound inclusive and upper-bound exclusive, so "1–4 years" means at least 1 year and under 5 years. This matches completed-age reporting.

    NNDSS transmits exact age and unit only. Age groups are derived downstream. Band definitions vary by report year. Verify flagged schemes against the current program documentation before production use.
